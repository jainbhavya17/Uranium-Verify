AT-10: actual cryptographic provenance tests. AT-11: room arithmetic/applicability. AT-12: evidence lineage and exact source navigation. AT-13: clarification/report/notification journey. AT-14: interpretable priority with officer rationale. AT-15: source filtering and abstention. AT-16: cross-tenant/assignment denial. AT-17: admin content restrictions and audited access changes. AT-18: automated route resolution plus partial manual browser/accessibility review; not claimed fully certified.
